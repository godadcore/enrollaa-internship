# Pilot guide — staff

1. Invite only approved pilot participants. Choose the correct **account type** and, for interns, one of the six official internship roles.
2. Confirm the invitation email was accepted by the provider. If delivery fails, the person does not have a working link.
3. Review onboarding submissions before treating someone as active.
4. Assign tasks only inside your department.
5. Check attendance and weekly reports each working day.
6. Do not paste invitation tokens, recovery links, or signed document URLs into Slack or email threads.
7. If authorization or privacy looks wrong, stop inviting and tell the programme admin immediately.
