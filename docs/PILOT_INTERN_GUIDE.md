# Pilot guide — interns

You will receive a private invitation link. Do not forward it.

1. Open the link and create your password.
2. Complete onboarding. Upload only the documents requested.
3. Sign in at the programme address you were given.
4. Check in each working day, and check out when you finish.
5. Open My Tasks, submit the deliverable URL, and read reviewer comments.
6. Use notifications for tasks and announcements.
7. Problems: message the staff contact named in your invitation email. Do not send passwords or bank details in that message.
